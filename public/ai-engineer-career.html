01 · Educational Pathway

How you get into the field

School (Class XI–XII)

  • Science stream with Mathematics — Maths is essential
  • Physics & Chemistry (PCM) for engineering routes
  • Opt for Computer Science / Informatics if offered
  • Start coding early — Python is a great first language

Entrance Exams

  • JEE Main & Advanced — IITs, NITs, IIITs
  • BITSAT — BITS Pilani campuses
  • CUET — central universities
  • State CETs & university-specific tests

Degree Routes

  • B.Tech / B.E. in CSE, AI & ML, or Data Science
  • B.Sc in Computer Science, Maths or Statistics
  • M.Tech / M.S. / PhD for research & senior roles
  • Online degrees & a strong project portfolio also count
  • Key point: There is no single fixed route. A solid grounding in mathematics and programming matters more than any one degree.

02 · Nature of Work

What an AI engineer actually does

Core Responsibilities

  • Design, train and fine-tune machine learning & deep learning models
  • Collect, clean and prepare data; engineer features
  • Deploy models into products and monitor them in production (MLOps)
  • Work on NLP, computer vision, recommendation & large language models
  • Write reliable, production-grade code and run experiments

Tools & Technologies

  • Languages: Python, SQL, sometimes C++ / Java
  • Frameworks: PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, Hugging Face
  • Cloud & DevOps: AWS, GCP, Azure, Docker, Git
  • Data: Pandas, NumPy, Spark, vector databases
  • Collaborates with data scientists, product & software teams

03 · Working Conditions

The work environment

Hours & Arrangements

  • Typical 40–45 hour work weeks
  • Extra hours near product launches or deadlines
  • Hybrid and remote work are common in tech
  • On-site work for robotics, hardware or defence projects

Teams & Culture

  • Small cross-functional teams (5–10 people)
  • Agile sprints, code reviews and stand-ups
  • Global collaboration across time zones
  • High screen time and a constant need to keep learning

04 · Personal Attributes Required

What helps you succeed

Technical Aptitude

  • Strong mathematics — linear algebra, probability, statistics
  • Logical reasoning & programming aptitude
  • Comfort with abstraction and problem solving

Mindset

  • Curiosity and self-driven learning
  • Patience for experimentation and debugging
  • Attention to detail and rigour with data

Soft Skills

  • Explaining complex ideas simply
  • Teamwork and collaboration
  • Ethical judgement and responsibility

05 · Career Progression Path

From entry level to leadership

Intern / TraineeStarting out
Learn on real projects, assist with data and experiments while completing studies.
Junior AI / ML Engineer0–2 years
Build and test models under guidance; strengthen coding and data skills.
AI / ML Engineer2–5 years
Own features end-to-end, deploy models to production, mentor juniors.
Senior AI Engineer5–8 years
Lead complex systems, set technical direction, review architecture.
Lead / Principal / ML Architect8–12 years
Design large-scale AI systems and guide multiple teams.
Head of AI / Director / CTO12+ years
Own AI strategy and organisation-wide impact.

The long-term relevance of AI

Emerging Trends
