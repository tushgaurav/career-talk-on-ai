Educational Pathway
Educational Pathway

Two ways into the field

There is no single fixed route. Pick the one that fits you, or blend both.

Route A

The degree route

Structured, credential first
  1. Science with Maths in Class XI and XIIAdd Computer Science if offered, and start Python early.
  2. Crack an entrance examJEE, BITSAT or CUET for IITs, NITs, IIITs and BITS.
  3. B.Tech or B.Sc in CSE, AI-ML or Data ScienceOptional M.Tech or PhD for research and senior roles.
  4. Internships, then a full time jobCampus placements and referrals open the first role.
Route B

The builder route

Self taught, portfolio first
  1. Learn one language deeplyMaster Python and the maths behind machine learning.
  2. Build real projectsShip them on GitHub and let the work speak for you.
  3. Find clients and freelanceTake paid gigs, small contracts and open source work.
  4. Improve, specialise, growTurn a real track record into full time and senior roles.
AI Engineer / Career Talk04
Nature of Work

What an AI engineer actually does

Core Responsibilities

  • Design, train and fine tune machine learning and deep learning models
  • Collect, clean and prepare data, then engineer features
  • Deploy models into products and monitor them in production
  • Work on NLP, computer vision, recommendations and large language models
  • Write reliable, production grade code and run experiments

Tools and Technologies

  • Languages. Python, SQL, sometimes C++ or Java
  • Frameworks. PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit learn, Hugging Face
  • Cloud and DevOps. AWS, GCP, Azure, Docker, Git
  • Data. Pandas, NumPy, Spark, vector databases
  • Collaborates with data scientists, product and software teams
AI Engineer / Career Talk05
Working Conditions and Attributes

The environment and what helps you succeed

Hours and Setup

  • Typical 40 to 45 hour work weeks
  • Extra hours near launches or deadlines
  • Hybrid and remote work are common
  • On site work for robotics and hardware

Teams and Culture

  • Small cross functional teams
  • Agile sprints, code reviews, stand ups
  • Global collaboration across time zones
  • Constant need to keep learning

Personal Attributes

  • Strong mathematics and logical reasoning
  • Curiosity and self driven learning
  • Patience for experimentation and debugging
  • Explaining complex ideas simply

Growth and reward

The path runs from intern to leadership. Compensation rises quickly with proven delivery and technical ownership.

Career Progression Path

From entry level to leadership

Intern or TraineeStarting out

Junior AI or ML Engineer0 to 2 years
Build and test models under guidance. Strengthen coding and data skills.
AI or ML Engineer2 to 5 years
Own features end to end, deploy models to production, mentor juniors.

Lead complex systems, set technical direction, review architecture.
Lead, Principal or ML Architect8 to 12 years
Design large scale AI systems and guide multiple teams.

Own AI strategy and organisation wide impact.
